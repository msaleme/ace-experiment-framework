# Commissioning pack: ACE evidence-literacy shorts

**Status:** ready for storyboard review.  
**Audience:** research, engineering, and internal enablement audiences.  
**Distribution:** internal review only. External distribution requires a separate editorial,
claims, confidentiality, and publishing decision.

## Purpose

Commission four 45-60 second, sound-off, vertical explainers showing how ACE keeps a completed
experiment's conclusions tied to retained evidence and declared claim scope. These are not
benchmark advertisements, performance claims, independent attestations, or product demos.

The public Token-Bleed R5 packet is the sole factual reference. Its boundary is non-negotiable:
ACE evaluates evidence that a runner has retained; it does not run the workload, recreate missing
rows, or independently attest to collection. R5 is endpoint- and model-scoped. It contains both
accepted governed-versus-full conclusions and rejected governed-versus-lexical conclusions under a
frozen cost condition.

## Batch

| Slug | Persona | Premise |
|---|---|---|
| `ace-contract-before-run` | Research lead | Freeze the question and claim boundaries before evidence exists. |
| `ace-retain-the-evidence` | Evaluation engineer | Retain contract, preflight, evidence, decision pack, and hashes together. |
| `ace-claim-by-claim` | Technical executive | One run can support a narrow result while rejecting a different claim. |
| `ace-verification-is-not-attestation` | Governance/risk leader | Rechecking a public packet is valuable but does not certify original collection. |

## Non-negotiable production rules

- Use the chips **REFERENCE APPLICATION**, **MODEL/ENDPOINT SCOPED**, and **NOT INDEPENDENT
  ATTESTATION** as appropriate. Do not call the packet “validated,” “certified,” or “proven.”
- On the claim-by-claim cut, show these exact R5 outcome classes only: accepted against raw full
  context; rejected against lexical filter under the frozen 3x prompt-cost ceiling. Do not add a
  numerical result, aggregate score, cross-model claim, or general governance claim.
- Do not show the private raw report, host identifiers, or derived private R4 material.
- Use an abstract experiment ledger, contract page, hash ribbon, and decision cards. Do not fake
  terminal output, customer dashboards, research citations, or third-party endorsements.
- No commercial CTA, booking language, pricing, install prompt, or request for a demo.

## Lexicon

**Required:** `contract`, `retained evidence`, `claim`, `scope`.

**Preferred:** `preflight`, `decision pack`, `hash`, `reference application`, `accepted`,
`rejected`, `endpoint-scoped`.

**Forbidden:** `certified`, `proven`, `validated result`, `independent verification`, `benchmark
winner`, `governance wins`, `ROI`, `savings`, `production result`, `cross-model`.

## Visual system

- **Palette:** paper-white and graphite, with cobalt for retained artifacts, green for accepted,
  amber for rejected, and neutral gray for boundary/caveat text.
- **Motifs:** sealed experiment contract, numbered evidence ledger, SHA-256 hash ribbon, split
  claim cards, a transparent boundary wall between ACE assessment and runner collection.
- **People:** optional anonymous silhouette only. The evidence record, not a person or machine,
  remains the hero.
- **Motion:** B1-B5 only, after storyboard approval. Keep B0/B6/B7 and cards graphic-first.

## CTA and gates

End card: **“A benchmark result is not evidence by itself.”**  
Subline: **“Retain the contract. Scope each claim.”**  
No CTA, link, handle, or external destination.

Required human gates: storyboard, script, claims, confidentiality, visual, delivery. Publishing is
out of scope for this commission and cannot be self-approved.
